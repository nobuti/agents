# Playbook: wayfinder

**Use when:** a huge, foggy effort (greenfield, or a feature too big for one session) where the way to the destination is not visible. Slow and dense: never for a well-scoped feature.

1. `/wayfinder`: chart the map of decision tickets; resolve them one at a time.
2. When the map clears, hand off at `/to-spec` (collapses the map's decisions into a plan). Then continue with the feature playbook from step 3. Build straight from the map only if the effort proved genuinely small (state why).

**Success evidence:** map URL or path; every decision ticket resolved or consciously deferred, listed; the spec produced by `/to-spec`.
